# pkDwich

pkDwich is a Nintendo Switch homebrew to view Pokemon recipes while playing Pokemon games. It supports Scarlet/Violet sandwiches, Sword/Shield curries, and Legends: ZA donuts.

### Features:

#### Sword & Shield

##### Curry Dex

- Full Curry Dex with 151 curries, numbered and categorized

#### Scarlet & Violet

##### Shiny -Sparkling Power Lv.3-

- Shiny Recipes (w/ Salty Herba Mystica)
- Shiny Recipes (w/ Alternative)

##### Power Effects -Lv.1 to Lv.3- (Simple Recipes)

- Egg Power

Sparkling Power & Encounter Power are already available in the Shiny Recipes (monotype Lv.3 only)

#### Legends: Z-A

##### Story Donuts

- Story Donuts recipes

## How do I use pkDwich?

1. Setup [WerWolv's Tesla](https://gbatemp.net/threads/tesla-the-nintendo-switch-overlay-menu.557362/) or [Ultrahand](https://github.com/ppkantorski/Ultrahand-Overlay) 
1. Download the latest `pkDwich.ovl`
1. Copy `pkDwich.ovl` to be inside the `/switch/.overlays` directory on your switch's SD card
1. Start your Pokemon game (Scarlet/Violet, Sword/Shield, or Legends: Z-A)
1. Press the key combo to open Tesla Menu (L + Dpad Down + R joystick as of this writing)
1. Open pkDwich with Tesla
1. Select your game from the menu to view the corresponding recipes
